- It's **feasible to measure latency for every experiment** with ElectroTime — no elaborate
  changes to your setup, and it can be applied to existing experiments.
- **Adjust your analysis:** shift epoch onset by the average latency, and reduce Type II error
  by modelling the latency variance in your inference.
- **Report latency and variance** in the literature to improve replicability and comparison.
- If the variance is too severe, **adapt your experimental setup or stimulation code**.
