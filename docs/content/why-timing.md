ERP and ERF analyses assume your triggers line up with the stimulus — but they don't.
Your software, operating system, monitor, graphics card and sound card each add a variable
latency, and this delay is different every time.

Tools that measure this cost $2000–3000 and are closed, single-purpose boxes.
ElectroTime does the same job for under $80, fully open.
